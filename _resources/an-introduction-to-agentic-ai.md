Explore this [collection of hands-on agentic AI resources](https://github.com/acceleratescience/hands-on-agentic-ai), created for researchers interested in applying agentic AI in their work.

AI agents bring together **agency**—the ability to act towards a goal—and **autonomy**—the ability to make decisions with limited human intervention. Together, these capabilities enable an agent to select appropriate actions, plan its next steps, and adjust its behaviour as new information or changing circumstances emerge.

Rather than producing a single response to a prompt, an AI agent can interpret a goal, break it down into manageable steps, select and use external tools, monitor its progress, and adapt its strategy based on the results. This iterative process allows agents to approach complex, multi-step tasks with greater flexibility and independence.

The workshop provides a practical introduction to building AI systems that go beyond individual language-model calls. The emphasis is on understanding how the components of an agentic system fit together and gaining hands-on experience by working directly with code.

The material is intended primarily for researchers and developers interested in applying agentic AI to scientific research.
